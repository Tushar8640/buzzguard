Multi-tool actions

Eight focused tools. Combine them freely.

Every rule can trigger one or multiple actions at once, with automatic specificity-based conflict resolution when rules overlap.

Cooldown

Let the first message chime, then silence rapid follow-ups from the same chat for 10s–300s.

Dismiss

Auto-swipe promos and spam immediately or after a short delay without muting the whole app.

Snooze & Batch

Hold noisy alerts and receive them together on schedule, with a 1-tap Deliver Now button.

Custom Alert

Assign distinct vibration patterns (Gentle, Double Tap, Heartbeat, Rapid, SOS) to VIP senders.

Remind Me

Keep gently reminding you on a set interval until an important notification is actually opened.

Alarm

Break through silence on the alarm channel with SOS vibration and a Stop Alarm button.

Auto-Reply

Send an automatic reply via Android RemoteInput if a message goes unseen past your threshold.

Secret

Replace sensitive OTPs and bank previews with a clean lock-screen-safe placeholder message.

Context & schedules

Filter by active hours, days of week (Sun–Sat), screen-off state, Wi-Fi connection, and ringer mode.

Undo swipe & local history

Accidentally swiped an alert? Restore it from the 6-second Undo prompt or searchable SQLite history.

Offline by architecture

Can't leak what it can't send.

Notification managers read sensitive messages. BuzzGuard doesn't ask you to trust a privacy policy — it omits Android's internet permission altogether.

✓
No INTERNET permission Android blocks network sockets at the OS level. Your notifications physically cannot leave your device.
✓
On-device SQLite storage Rules and up to 500 history entries live strictly inside local app storage with zero cloud sync.
✓
No ads, no analytics, no accounts Install, grant notification access, and toggle your first rule in under 30 seconds.
